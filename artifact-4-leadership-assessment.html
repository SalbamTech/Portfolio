Artifact 04 · Leading change

AI/ML Integration Leadership Skills Self-Assessment

A structured evaluation of the technical and change-leadership competencies needed to guide responsible AI/ML integration.

  • Change leadership
  • Self-assessment
  • Stakeholder engagement
  • Continuous development
Course context
AIML-500 · Discussion 1.5 — Personal Assessment: Leading Change
Artifact format
Structured self-assessment with written reflection
Primary audience
Executive coaches, HR leaders, and technical-leadership peers
Introduction

A self-assessment of leadership competencies for AI/ML integration and organizational change.

Objective

Critically evaluate my leadership capabilities and identify a concrete development path.

Process

Complete the instrument, analyze the profile, reflect, and connect findings to development.

Tools & tech

Self-assessment instrument, reflective analysis, and Microsoft Word.

Description

AI integration requires technical and human leadership.

The assessment examines the competencies required to lead AI/ML initiatives, including enough technical understanding to guide teams and the change-leadership ability to help organizations adopt new workflows responsibly.

Vision casting

Connect emerging capabilities to a clear, responsible organizational direction.

Stakeholder engagement

Listen, communicate, and build alignment across groups affected by change.

Risk management

Recognize technical, operational, ethical, and adoption risks early.

Tangible artifact

What the assessment surfaced

Established strengths

  • Systems thinking
  • Ethical reasoning
  • Stakeholder communication
  • Risk-aware technical judgment

Development priorities

  • Cross-functional team building
  • Rapid AI/ML prototyping
  • Continued technical depth
  • Leading organizational learning
Reflective principleSelf-assessment is useful when it creates a development plan, not when it is treated as a final or objective definition of capability.

Process

From instrument to development plan

  1. Complete honestlyRespond to the standardized competency instrument without inflating the result.
  2. ScoreReview the profile across leadership dimensions.
  3. CompareConnect the results with evidence from my professional experience.
  4. ReflectIdentify strengths, gaps, and assumptions in the instrument.
  5. PlanTranslate the findings into specific areas for continued growth.
  6. RevisitUse reflection and future evidence to update the development path.

Value proposition of the artifact

Why this work matters

Unique value

This artifact demonstrates honest self-reflection, emotional intelligence, and a commitment to continuous development as a technical leader.

Organizational relevance

Self-aware leaders are better positioned to create psychologically safe teams and navigate the human dimensions of AI adoption.
